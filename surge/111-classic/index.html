    111 (Classic) 🦁¶

    Best For: Players who enjoy building up to one massive, satisfying hit.

    Tradeoff: All your eggs are in one basket (Surge) + Maelstrom management.

    • Powerful burst windows with the Breaking Moon combo.
    • No need to hold Counter, it charges up to two stacks.
    • Very high gem efficiency: Surge is nearly all of your DPS.
    • Accessible from zero Ark Grid cores with minor adjustments.
    • Must constantly balance Surge back attack rate with Surge CPM.

    Ark Setup¶

    TipArk Passive
    • Use the Ark Passive Calculator to optimize Evolution nodes.
    • Release Potential 3 is taken because downtime, raid phasing, or death can occasionally enable an extra use.
      • The alternative is Transcendent Power 3, which is only really useful for guardians or your fourth non-gold HW raid.
    NoteArk Grid
    • You can level Ark Grid cores to your preference, but 17p Surge Core grants a second Earth Cleaver stack. This frees up a gem slot and allows you to cast Earth Cleaver without needing to hold it for an upcoming raid mechanic.

    Skill Setup¶

    TipRunes
    • Use Purify on Spincutter if needed.
    • Focus on Maelstrom + wine handles most mana needs, since Breaking Moon cycles restore it.
      • Use mana food instead if you don't trust your or your support's uptime (spec bards), or for trixion-like content.
    • Alternatively, Legendary Bleed on Maelstrom + mana food: higher ceiling/lower floor, even with Raid Captain.
    NoteOptions & Tripods
    • You can use the Weak Point Detection tripod on Blade Dance.
      • Requires Lv 10 CD gem and/or raid downtime for it not to become a bottleneck.
      • You must swap Wind Cut CD gem to Blade Dance CD, and it's a marginal DPS increase.
    • Leap Attack vs Earth Explosion tripod on Earth Cleaver is up to personal preference.
      • Earth Explosion is faster and may raise rotation consistency with an extra stack.
      • Leap Attack is slower but increases overall DPS by ~1% and offers more mobility.
    • Dark Axel (1-1-2) can be used instead of Spincutter, but offers no recovery.

    Rotation¶

    There's an optimal skill order, but you have flexibility when facing downtime or weaving in mobility skills.

    Breaking Moon grants 60 stacks on hit and empowers your next Surge with +60% Critical Damage.

    Spincutter is your mobility skill and backup stack builder. Use it to guarantee a back attack on Surge.

    Use the Breaking Moon cycle and its follow-up when available, then repeat the regular cycle otherwise.

    From 3 orbs:

    1Breaking Moon Cycle + Follow-Up
    Alternative · Awakening Follow-Up
    2Regular Cycle×2
    1. In the follow-up cycles: cast Maelstrom if you have 2 stacks, otherwise just continue without it.
    2. Do NOT worry about Raid Captain efficiency; Keen Blunt Weapon is just as inefficient or worse!
      • Breaking Moon's Critical Damage bonus to the next Surge (biggest hit) is additive to Keen Blunt Weapon.
      • Raid Captain fully buffs the empowered Surge and can make use of Rage/Atropine for the follow-up.
    3. Blade Assault scales much worse on Surge than on Remaining Energy and takes too long to cast.
      • It's still useful for Atropine openers, or it can be saved to greed with push immunity/Hyper Awakening.
    4. It seems more complicated than it really is, watch this video to see how a full rotation plays out.
    AltStack Reserve Rotation

    An alternative to the default rotation. It builds a stack reserve, so you're never short on them. Goes 1>2>2>3>1, etc.

    1Breaking Moon Cycle
    2Regular Cycle×2
    3Pre-Breaking Moon Cycle

    From zero orbs:

    1. Use a Stimulant (recommended) or proceed to #2.
      • Rat Pack: Use Maelstrom's Orb Control tripod and Flash Blink awakening.
    2. Generate one orb, build at least 40 stacks, then Surge to refill all 3 orbs.

    Atropine usage:

    1. Fit three Surges into a 10 second window. Use Atropine right before the first Surge hits.
    2. The second or third Surge must be part of a Breaking Moon cycle or you won't make it.
    3. Stacks and the environment vary, so a fixed rotation would just be a shackle.
